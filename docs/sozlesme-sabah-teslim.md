**MORNING CLIPS SERVICE AGREEMENT**

Between **[Your business name]** ("Editor") and **[Channel name / legal name]** ("Creator").

**1. Service.** Each day the Creator streams, the Editor reviews the stream recording and delivers up to [5] vertical video clips with captions by [09:00] in the Creator's time zone. Clips are delivered as files for the Creator's approval.

**2. Source material.** The Creator grants the Editor a limited, non-exclusive permission to download and edit the Creator's own stream recordings solely to provide this service. The Creator provides recordings by download link, or confirms in writing that the Editor may download them from the Creator's channel. The Editor deletes source recordings within [14] days of delivery.

**3. Publishing.** The Editor never publishes clips. Only the Creator decides whether a clip is posted, on the Creator's own accounts. The Creator owns the delivered clips.

**4. Content rules.** Because clips may contain third-party game footage, every clip follows these rules. The Editor will not deliver, and the Creator agrees not to request, clips that:
   a. show isolated story cutscenes, compilations of cutscenes or plot dialogue, or the game's ending;
   b. contain pre-release or leaked material;
   c. show or teach cheats, glitches for in-game money, mod menus or account sales;
   d. use game footage to promote a product, brand or sponsor;
   e. are mainly game footage without the Creator's own commentary, face cam or reaction.
   Each clip features the Creator's commentary or reaction as its main content.

**5. Music.** The Editor removes or replaces licensed in-game music where it is clearly audible and not essential to the moment, to reduce copyright claims. The Editor does not guarantee that a clip will not receive a claim.

**6. Takedowns and policy changes.** If a rights holder (including the game publisher) issues a takedown or changes its policy, the Editor will adjust the content rules within [2] business days and the Creator may cancel immediately with a pro-rated refund.

**7. Fees.** $[150] per month, paid in advance. Either party may cancel with [7] days' notice. No refund for clips already delivered.

**8. No guarantees.** The Editor does not guarantee views, followers, revenue or platform approval.

**9. Liability.** Each party is responsible for its own compliance with platform terms. The Editor's total liability is limited to the fees paid in the previous [1] month.

**10. Confidentiality and data.** The Editor uses the Creator's materials and channel data only to provide the service and does not share them with third parties.

**11. Governing law.** [To be decided with counsel.]
